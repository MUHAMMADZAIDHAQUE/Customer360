# Customer360 - Canonical Metrics & KPI Definitions

This document establishes the **authoritative, single source of truth** definitions and mathematical formulas for all key performance indicators (KPIs) in the Customer360 analytics engineering layer.

Every dbt model, analytical query, and downstream dashboard **must strictly follow these definitions**. Contradictory formulas across models or documentation are strictly forbidden.

---

## 1. Core Platform Metrics

### 1.1 Churn Rate (Account Level)
* **Definition**: The proportion of distinct customers active at the start of an observation period (or cohort) who officially terminated or cancelled their subscription during that period.
* **Canonical Formula**:
  $$\text{Churn Rate} = \frac{\text{Count of Churned Customers in Period}}{\text{Count of Active Customers at Start of Period}} \times 100$$
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  ROUND(COUNT(DISTINCT churned_customer_id) * 100.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT active_customer_id), 0), 2)
  ```
* **Boundary Rules**:
  * Churn must be evaluated against the start-of-period customer base to avoid distortion from newly acquired customers in the same period.
  * Involuntary churn (payment delinquency) and voluntary churn (cancellation) are subsets that sum to total churn.

---

### 1.2 Retention Rate
* **Definition**: The percentage of customers who continue their subscription over an observation window.
* **Canonical Formula**:
  $$\text{Retention Rate} = 100\% - \text{Churn Rate} = \frac{\text{Count of Retained Customers at End of Period}}{\text{Count of Active Customers at Start of Period}} \times 100$$
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  ROUND(100.0 - (COUNT(DISTINCT churned_customer_id) * 100.0 / NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT active_customer_id), 0)), 2)
  ```

---

### 1.3 Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)
* **Definition**: The normalized monthly subscription revenue generated by all active paying customer subscriptions.
* **Canonical Formula**:
  * For monthly contracts: $\text{MRR} = \text{monthly\_price}$
  * For annual contracts: $\text{MRR} = \frac{\text{annual\_price}}{12}$
  * Total Platform MRR:
  $$\text{Total MRR} = \sum_{i \in \text{Active Subscriptions}} \text{monthly\_price}_i$$
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  SUM(CASE WHEN subscription_status = 'active' THEN monthly_price ELSE 0 END)
  ```
* **Boundary Rules**:
  * Cancelled, expired, or past-due subscriptions contribute **$0.00** to active MRR.
  * One-off setup fees or refunds do not affect MRR (they are tracked in cash collections).

---

### 1.4 Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
* **Definition**: The annualized value of all active recurring subscription agreements.
* **Canonical Formula**:
  $$\text{ARR} = \text{MRR} \times 12 = \sum_{i \in \text{Active Subscriptions}} (\text{monthly\_price}_i \times 12)$$
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  SUM(CASE WHEN subscription_status = 'active' THEN monthly_price * 12.0 ELSE 0 END)
  ```

---

### 1.5 Average Revenue Per User (ARPU)
* **Definition**: The average recurring revenue generated by each active paying subscriber per month.
* **Canonical Formula**:
  $$\text{ARPU} = \frac{\text{Total Active MRR}}{\text{Total Active Paying Customers}}$$
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  ROUND(SUM(CASE WHEN subscription_status = 'active' THEN monthly_price ELSE 0 END) * 1.0 / 
        NULLIF(COUNT(DISTINCT CASE WHEN subscription_status = 'active' THEN customer_id END), 0), 2)
  ```

---

### 1.6 Customer Lifetime Value (CLV / LTV)
* **Definition**: The total net commercial value an account generates over its entire relationship lifespan.
* **Historical CLV (Realized)**:
  $$\text{Realized CLV} = \sum \text{Successful Transaction Amounts for Customer}$$
* **Predictive / Modelled CLV**:
  $$\text{Expected CLV} = \frac{\text{Customer MRR} \times \text{Gross Margin \%}}{\text{Monthly Churn Hazard Rate}}$$
  *Assuming standard SaaS gross margin of 80% and account-specific churn hazard.*
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  -- Historical Realized Lifetime Value:
  COALESCE(SUM(CASE WHEN payment_status = 'succeeded' THEN amount ELSE 0 END), 0)
  ```

---

### 1.7 Revenue at Risk
* **Definition**: The total annualized recurring revenue (ARR) associated with currently active customer subscriptions that display acute churn signals.
* **Acute Churn Criteria**:
  1. Average support ticket satisfaction score $\le 2.5$ **OR**
  2. $\ge 3$ support tickets submitted in trailing 60 days **OR**
  3. Weekly active sessions declining by $> 50\%$ over previous 4 weeks **OR**
  4. At least 1 failed billing transaction in trailing 30 days.
* **Canonical Formula**:
  $$\text{Revenue at Risk} = \sum_{k \in \text{At-Risk Active Accounts}} (\text{monthly\_price}_k \times 12)$$
* **SQL Formulation**:
  ```sql
  SUM(CASE 
        WHEN subscription_status = 'active' AND is_at_risk = TRUE 
        THEN monthly_price * 12.0 
        ELSE 0 
      END)
  ```

## 2. Dimensional Definitions

| Dimension | Permitted Values | Definition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Contract Type** | `monthly`, `annual`, `multi_year` | Contractual renewal cadence commitment |
| **Plan Tier** | `Starter`, `Growth`, `Professional`, `Enterprise` | Feature and seat package |
| **Customer Status** | `active`, `churned`, `paused` | High-level account lifecycle state |
| **Subscription Status** | `active`, `cancelled`, `past_due`, `expired` | Billing contract state |
| **Churn Reason** | `price_sensitivity`, `competitor_switch`, `lack_of_features`, `poor_support`, `infrequent_use`, `payment_delinquency` | Primary exit driver |
| **Churn Type** | `voluntary`, `involuntary` | Customer initiated vs. payment failure |
